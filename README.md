## 🔥 Lead Scoring Explained

Your leads are scored based on:

| What We Check | Points | Why It Matters |
|---------------|--------|----------------|
| Budget (Do they have money?) | 30 pts | Big budgets = serious buyer |
| Timeline (How urgent?) | 25 pts | ASAP = ready to buy NOW |
| Company type | 15 pts | Right industry = better fit |
| Complete info | 10 pts | More details = serious inquiry |
| Referral? | +10 pts | Referred leads are warmer |

**Score of 70+?** = HOT LEAD ✅ (Slack alert + email)
**Score of 50-69?** = WARM LEAD 🔥 (Email nurture)
**Score below 50?** = COLD LEAD ❄️ (Save for later)
