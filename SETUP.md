# How to Set Up Your Lead Qualification

## What You Need
- Airtable account (free)
- Gmail account
- Slack account
- Make.com account (free)

## Step 1: Airtable Setup

Go to airtable.com and create these fields in your table:
- First Name
- Last Name
- Email
- Company
- Budget
- Timeline
- Qualification
- Status
- Contacted on

## Step 2: Make.com Setup

1. Go to make.com
2. Import your scenario (upload the JSON file)
3. Connect your apps:
   - Airtable
   - Gmail
   - Slack

## Step 3: Test It

Add a test lead to Airtable with:
- Name: Test
- Email: your email
- Budget: High
- Timeline: ASAP

Then check:
- Did Slack send a message?
- Did you get an email?
